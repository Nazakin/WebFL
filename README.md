# WebFL Sequencer

A little drum machine that runs in your browser — a pet project inspired by FL Studio.

Pick a beat, click steps on a 16-step grid, and it plays back a loop with kicks, snares, hi-hats, claps, toms, crashes and more. Every sound is synthesized live, nothing is a recorded sample.

## What you can do

- Build a beat on a 16-step grid, any number of tracks
- Add tracks and pick a sound from a dropdown
- Mute and adjust volume per track
- Hit Play/Stop, change the BPM
- Load ready-made patterns: Drum & Bass, Techno, and a custom one called Neon Drift
- Click a step to hear what it sounds like
- Switch between light and dark theme

Nothing is saved — refreshing the page gives you a clean slate.
